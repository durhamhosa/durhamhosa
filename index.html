Who we are

More than an interest in healthcare.

Durham HOSA brings students together to explore the many paths within healthcare and develop skills that can help them in school, future studies, and beyond.

Whether you're curious about medicine, research, public health, or another healthcare profession, HOSA offers ways to learn, connect, and challenge yourself.
